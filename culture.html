Our Corporate Culture

Building a culture of excellence, innovation, and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Purpose

Mission Statement

To deliver exceptional logistics solutions that empower businesses and enrich lives through innovative technology, reliable service, and unwavering commitment to excellence. We strive to be the most trusted delivery partner for businesses across China and beyond.

Our Vision

Vision

To become the leading integrated logistics service provider in Asia, recognized for our technological innovation, operational excellence, and sustainable practices. We envision a future where seamless delivery is a reality for every business and individual.

Core Values

The Principles That Guide Us

Integrity

We conduct business with the highest ethical standards, ensuring transparency and honesty in all our dealings.

Excellence

We pursue excellence in everything we do, continuously improving our services and exceeding expectations.

Reliability

Our customers trust us to deliver on time, every time. We take this responsibility seriously.

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. Their success is our success.

Innovation

Sustainability

We are committed to sustainable practices that protect our environment for future generations.

Our Journey

Company History

2015

Company Founded

Beijing Dingsheng Huatang Delivery Service Co., Ltd. was established with a vision to transform urban logistics in China.

2017

Mobile App Launch

Launched our first mobile application, bringing same-city food delivery services to thousands of users.

2019

Expansion & Growth

Expanded our service offerings to include freight forwarding and supply chain management solutions.

2021

Technology Innovation

Introduced AI-powered route optimization and real-time tracking systems for enhanced efficiency.

2024

Market Leadership

Established as a leading provider of integrated logistics solutions with nationwide coverage.
